我们将电子准费米电势计算式两边同时对 x 求导，
![[电子准费米电势计算式]]
$$
\frac{d\psi_{n}}{dx} = \frac{d\psi_{e}}{dx}-\frac{kT}{q}\frac{n_{e}}{n}\frac{1}{n_{e}}\frac{dn}{dx}
$$
然后进行移项，
$$
\begin{aligned}
-qn_{p}\mu_{e}\frac{d\psi_{e}}{dx}&=-qn_{p}\mu_{e}\frac{d\psi_{e}}{dx}+qn_{p}\mu_{e}\frac{kT}{q}\frac{1}{n_{p}}\frac{dn_{e}}{dx}\\
&=qn_{p}\mu_{e}E+\mu_{s}kT\frac{dn_{p}}{dx}\\
\end{aligned}
$$
用爱因斯坦关系，
$$
-qn_{p}\mu_{e}\frac{d\phi_{e}}{dx} = qn_{p}\mu_{e}E+qD_{s}\frac{dn_{p}}{dx}= J_{n}(x)
$$
